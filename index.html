Beef Bones

Meaty beef bones slowly simmered over a traditional charcoal fire, allowing their natural fats and rich flavours to create a hearty, full-bodied broth without the addition of cooking oil. Served with your choice of white or brown sadza and freshly cooked tsunga vegetables for an authentic and satisfying Zimbabwean meal.

US$10.00

Road Runner Chicken

A rich and flavourful free-range chicken, slow-cooked until tender in a traditional home-style sauce. Served with your choice of white or brown sadza, or rice, for a wholesome and satisfying meal inspired by authentic Zimbabwean flavours.

A hearty Zimbabwean favourite featuring tender, slow-cooked beef simmered in a rich, flavourful gravy and served with freshly prepared sadza and your choice of rape or covo vegetables. A comforting, home-style meal inspired by the authentic flavours of Highfields Town in Harare.
